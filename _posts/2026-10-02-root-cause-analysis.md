## Summary

A group of people attempted to find flaws in Kayne. This went poorly.

## Timeline

- **T+0:** Someone says "nobody is that perfect"
- **T+1 week:** Audit begins
- **T+1 month:** Zero mistakes found
- **T+2 months:** Auditors begin taking notes on how to be more like Kayne
- **T+now:** Blog exists

## Root cause

Kayne is just like that.

## Action items

- [x] Accept that Kayne is like that
- [x] Write a blog about it
- [ ] Make fewer mistakes ourselves (in progress, forever)

## Blameless note

In keeping with good postmortem culture, nobody is to blame. Except maybe Kayne, for being this good. We love him.
