The audit is going great. We found loads of mistakes. Unfortunately, all of them are ours.

## Monday

**Mistake:** Ran `chmod -R 777` on the lab share "just to test something."
**Kayne's fix:** Restored the permissions, then explained the principle of least privilege so nicely that nobody felt bad.

## Wednesday

**Mistake:** Pushed an API key to a public repo.
**Kayne's fix:** Rotated the key in under five minutes and showed us how to set up a pre-commit secret scanner.

## Friday

**Mistake:** Scheduled the club meeting in a room that doesn't exist.
**Kayne's fix:** Already had a backup room booked. Of course he did.
