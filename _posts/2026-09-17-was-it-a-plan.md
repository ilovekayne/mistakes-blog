For one glorious afternoon, we thought we had him.

Kayne let a CTF timer run out on a challenge he was clearly about to solve. We wrote "MISTAKE #1" on the whiteboard in huge letters.

Then we found out why.

A newer member was one step away from solving it on their own, and Kayne stepped back so they could get the flag first. Their first ever solve.

We erased the whiteboard.

> **Audit finding:** Not a mistake. Possibly the opposite of a mistake.

The count remains at zero.
